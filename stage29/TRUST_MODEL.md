# Stage 29 design lock and trust model

## Design-set diagnosis

Stage 28 is the only dataset used to select the Stage-29 modification.

Observed Stage-28 combined misses:

    15 / 96 episodes

Exploratory characteristics:

    short duration:        13 / 15
    status substitution:   10 / 15
    low density:            9 / 15
    multiplicity one:      15 / 15

The modification is frozen before any Stage-29 private schedule is realized.

## Frozen rule

Let A be the verified attestation projection and C_t the consumer projection.

    I_t = 1[
        verified(A)
        AND (
            count(A) != count(C_t)
            OR semantic(A) != semantic(C_t)
        )
    ]

The legacy detector remains D_t.

    D'_t = D_t OR I_t

No numeric parameter is estimated from Stage 28.

## Negative control

For the common-source branch the trusted side is the colluding witness and is
explicitly marked as not being a verified attestation reference.

Therefore the Stage-29 invariant channel is disabled there.

This prevents the new rule from silently treating an untrusted common-mode
observer as an attestation oracle.

## Held-out protocol

Stage 29 uses fresh runner identifiers 9601..9608 and fresh private schedules
generated by the unchanged Stage-28 distribution.

The realized schedules remain unavailable to:

- the authority job;
- the attestation projection job;
- the blind detector jobs.

Labels are joined only during the final audit.

## Remaining limitation

A one-sample invariant mismatch is only justified when the signed baseline is
both trustworthy and current.

If legitimate runtime scaling, deregistration, or state transitions occur
without a corresponding signed baseline update, this channel can generate false
alarms. Stage 29 therefore validates a static-baseline laboratory case, not a
complete production freshness protocol.
